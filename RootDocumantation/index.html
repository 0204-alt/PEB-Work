DESIGN BASIS

Wind: Vz = Vb × k1 × k2 × k3 Wind pressure: pz = 0.6 × Vz² Frame tributary width = bay spacing Member selection: independent column/rafter demand screening for DL + LL + wind; crane effects only on crane-supporting columns. Section screening considers axial + bending, slenderness/effective-length assumptions and deflection/serviceability triggers. Connection geometry is standardized where practical for bolting interchangeability. Applicable Indian Standards and final STAAD analysis govern final design.
For final structural design, code clauses, load combinations, section stability, buckling, serviceability, connections, crane loads and foundations must be checked by a qualified structural engineer.
